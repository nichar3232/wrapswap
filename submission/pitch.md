# Unison — pitch

**Tagline:** Share-for-share conversion, no cash leg: one Uniswap v4 hook settles every swap between wrappers of the same stock.

## Pitch (≤120 words)

The same Apple share now trades through different issuers, such as Coinbase's tokenized AAPL and Backed's xStocks AAPLx. Each wrapper is its own island: switching issuers means selling for cash and buying back. Unison converts share for share, with no cash leg. A Uniswap v4 hook fills swaps from its ERC-6909 inventory at the issuers' share ratio, for a 2 bps base fee plus a skew fee only on trades that deepen its inventory imbalance, all to the LP. Dark Cross crosses sealed orders at the oracle mid and fills residuals through the same hook. Send pays in shares confidentially via Sui. Users only ever hold real issuer securities, gated to non-US users by Coinbase's Verified Country attestation.

## Why this is a Uniswap stack contribution

- **A generic wrapper-parity hook primitive.** ParityHook runs on any pool of two wrappers whose adapters report the same underlying. It works for any issuer pair, not just AAPL. The hook rejects a pool unless the key uses the dynamic-fee flag and both tokens map to one underlying ([`ParityHook.beforeInitialize` (ParityHook.sol#L223)](../contracts/src/ParityHook.sol#L223)). Pricing comes from each adapter's shares-per-token ratio and health flag ([`IWrapperAdapter.sharesPerToken` (IWrapperAdapter.sol#L24)](../contracts/src/interfaces/IWrapperAdapter.sol#L24), [`IWrapperAdapter.health` (IWrapperAdapter.sol#L25)](../contracts/src/interfaces/IWrapperAdapter.sol#L25); DECISIONS.md: "Adapters (IWrapperAdapter) expose sharesPerToken per whole token (1e18) plus a paused/stale health flag").
- **A settlement-engine pattern for custom-accounting hooks.** `beforeSwapReturnDelta` fills all-or-nothing from hook-owned ERC-6909 claims. Otherwise a zero delta falls through to concentrated liquidity on the same pool, and `afterSwap` enforces a 50 bps peg guard ([`ParityHook.beforeSwap` (ParityHook.sol#L245)](../contracts/src/ParityHook.sol#L245), [`ParityHook.afterSwap` (ParityHook.sol#L297)](../contracts/src/ParityHook.sol#L297); DECISIONS.md: "Fills are all-or-nothing from inventory via beforeSwapReturnDelta…"). Every fill uses one fee schedule, base + skew pips, through the v4 LP-fee override (DECISIONS.md: "final fee model").
- **A dark-cross residual settled into the lit pool inside `unlock`.** A commit-reveal batch crosses at an oracle mid. The uncrossed remainder is swapped into the ParityHook pool in the same `PoolManager.unlock` and fills from hook inventory at the same base + skew fee as any other swap; whatever inventory can't fill is refunded ([`DarkCrossHook.settle` (DarkCrossHook.sol#L262)](../contracts/src/DarkCrossHook.sol#L262), [`DarkCrossHook.unlockCallback` (DarkCrossHook.sol#L297)](../contracts/src/DarkCrossHook.sol#L297); DECISIONS.md: "DarkCrossHook … routes residuals into the ParityHook pool inside the same unlock").
