THE PROTOCOL

Three primitives, one neutral layer

Generic by design: any wrapper pair on one underlying — WBTC/cbBTC today, every future stock issuer next.

Custom accounting

ParityHook

Cancels the core swap with specified / unspecified deltas and fills at share-parity from hook inventory, on dynamic-fee pools with an on-chain fee override.

Roadmap
RISK-FREE PATH

One signed order, issuer to issuer

01
Wrap

Mint to canonical

Deposit any registered issuer's token and mint uAAPL 1:1 per underlying share. Minting carries no fee; the vault enforces full backing after every mutation.

02
Swap

Fill at share-parity

The ParityHook fills wrapper-to-wrapper swaps at exact parity from its own ERC-6909 inventory, with a peg guard rejecting deviations above 50 bps.

03
Cross

Batch or route residual

Larger flow enters the DarkCrossHook: sealed orders batch, cross at the oracle midpoint, and any residual routes to the lit pool in the same transaction.

BUILT ON UNISWAP v4

Real contracts, not mocked

Direct IHooks implementations with mined hook addresses, ERC-6909 claims accounting, and PositionManager-initialized pools on the Base fork.

PoolManager

The core v4 singleton, run unmodified on the Base mainnet fork.

ERC-6909 claims

Hook-owned inventory settles ERC-20 and mints claim tokens for parity fills.

TWAP oracle

A 64-observation accumulator yields the 30-minute midpoint for dark crosses.

PositionManager

Initializes pools and seeds concentrated liquidity through the official periphery.
