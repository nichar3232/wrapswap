# WrapSwap — pitch

**Tagline:** Share-for-share conversion, no USDC leg: one Uniswap v4 hook settles every swap between wrappers of the same stock.

## Pitch (≤120 words)

The same Apple share now trades through different issuers, such as Coinbase's tokenized AAPL on Base and Backed's xStocks AAPLx. Each wrapper is its own island. Switching issuers means selling into USDC and buying back. WrapSwap is a Uniswap v4 hook that converts share-for-share, no USDC leg. It fills swaps from its ERC-6909 inventory at the issuers' share ratio. It prices inventory skew into a dynamic fee, and it adds an off-hours risk premium while NYSE is closed. Swaps its inventory can't cover fall through to the same pool's liquidity under a peg guard. Dark-cross residuals settle through that same hook. Users only ever hold real issuer securities. Swaps are gated to non-US users by Coinbase's Verified Country attestation.

## Why this is a Uniswap stack contribution

- **A generic wrapper-parity hook primitive.** ParityHook runs on any pool of two wrappers whose adapters report the same underlying. It works for any issuer pair, not just AAPL. The hook rejects a pool unless the key uses the dynamic-fee flag and both tokens map to one underlying ([`ParityHook.beforeInitialize` (ParityHook.sol#L204)](../contracts/src/ParityHook.sol#L204)). Pricing comes from each adapter's shares-per-token ratio and health flag ([`IWrapperAdapter.sharesPerToken` (IWrapperAdapter.sol#L24)](../contracts/src/interfaces/IWrapperAdapter.sol#L24), [`IWrapperAdapter.health` (IWrapperAdapter.sol#L25)](../contracts/src/interfaces/IWrapperAdapter.sol#L25); DECISIONS.md: "Adapters (IWrapperAdapter) expose sharesPerToken per whole token (1e18) plus a paused/stale health flag").
- **A settlement-engine pattern for custom-accounting hooks.** `beforeSwapReturnDelta` fills all-or-nothing from hook-owned ERC-6909 claims. Otherwise a zero delta falls through to concentrated liquidity on the same pool, and `afterSwap` enforces a 50 bps peg guard ([`ParityHook.beforeSwap` (ParityHook.sol#L226)](../contracts/src/ParityHook.sol#L226), [`ParityHook.afterSwap` (ParityHook.sol#L279)](../contracts/src/ParityHook.sol#L279); DECISIONS.md: "Fills are all-or-nothing from inventory via beforeSwapReturnDelta…"). Every fill uses one fee schedule: base, skew and closed-market pips through the v4 LP-fee override (DECISIONS.md: "Fee is expressed in pips…").
- **A dark-cross residual settled into the lit pool inside `unlock`.** A commit-reveal batch crosses at an oracle mid. The uncrossed remainder is swapped into the ParityHook pool in the same `PoolManager.unlock`, so it gets the same parity fill, fee and peg guard as any other swap ([`DarkCrossHook.settle` (DarkCrossHook.sol#L246)](../contracts/src/DarkCrossHook.sol#L246), [`DarkCrossHook.unlockCallback` (DarkCrossHook.sol#L281)](../contracts/src/DarkCrossHook.sol#L281); DECISIONS.md: "DarkCrossHook … routes residuals into the ParityHook pool inside the same unlock").
